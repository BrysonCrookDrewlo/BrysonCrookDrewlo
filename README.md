I build the internal software that runs day-to-day operations at **Drewlo Holdings**, a property management and construction company. My work spans self-hosted AI, internal platforms, and the data and automation behind the business.

B.Eng. Software Engineering, Western University (2025). Most of my work lives in private repositories.

## What I work on

- **Self-hosted AI.** Speech, language models and assistant tools that run on our own hardware, keeping company data in-house.
- **Internal platforms.** A suite of containerized services that connect business systems through authenticated APIs.
- **Data and reporting.** Pipelines and dashboards that turn construction and property data into cost allocation and day-to-day reporting.
- **Workflow automation.** Email triage, routing and task coordination, with AI doing the sorting and people making the calls.
- **Full-stack apps.** Web and mobile apps for residents and staff, from database to interface.

## How I work

- **Data stays in-house.** If a system can run on our own hardware, it does.
- **Missing data is reported, not guessed.** When the answer isn't in the data, the software says so.
- **Failures are loud.** A broken dependency raises an error instead of falling back silently.
